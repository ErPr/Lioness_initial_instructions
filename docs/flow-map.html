Lioness · Collective-Mind Capture

A link on your phone becomes living evidence on a movement's tree.

Members feed the collective mind from anywhere. Lioness enriches each item, routes it onto the right goal, waits for one confirming tap, and lights up where attention is flowing. Six stages, one private-until-confirmed pipeline.

5 phases Next.js 16 · Prisma · SQLite branch claude/new-session-332a0x mock router · no API key needed
01
Phase 1 · CAPTURE

Share it from anywhere

A member shares a link, tweet, TikTok, or screenshot to Lioness from their phone's share sheet. It POSTs to /api/capture and lands in a private inbox in under two seconds — no fetching, no AI, just saved.

new pending
app/api/capture · lib/capture.ts
02
Phase 2 · ENRICH

The worker fills in the blanks

A background worker wakes every few seconds, fetches each item's metadata — oEmbed first, then OpenGraph, then the share-sheet text — so a bare URL becomes a titled, described, thumbnailed card. It never blocks and never gets stuck.

pending→enriched
lib/worker.ts · lib/enrich.ts
03
Phase 3 · ROUTE

AI decides where it belongs

Each item is matched against every movement's goal tree — by an on-device keyword router by default, or claude-sonnet-4-6 when a key is set — producing the top-3 candidate nodes with confidence. Two people sharing the same link fold into one canonical item, share-count 2.

enriched→routed
lib/route.ts · lib/treeDump.ts
04
Phase 4 · CONFIRM

One tap makes it real

The suggestion is just a suggestion until the owner taps Confirm. That creates an ordinary Post on the chosen node — the exact same path as a hand-added link — or they pick a different node, or reject. Nothing goes public without the tap.

routed→confirmed
lib/actions/capture.ts
05
Phase 4 · EVIDENCE

It becomes living evidence

The confirmed item now sits in the node's Top Links flyout tab, indistinguishable from a link a member added by hand. It's votable; ratify/contest math applies; an audit-log entry records where it came from.

confirmed→public Post
lib/treeQuery.ts · TreeCanvas
06
Phase 5 · HEAT

The map shows where the mind is pointing

Every routed and confirmed item is a unit of attention. Nodes glow — share-count-weighted, decaying over 7 days — and a “Where attention is flowing” panel ranks the hottest. Hot and quiet nodes read at a glance.

reads all recent evidence
lib/heat.ts · AttentionPanel

The item's lifecycle

pending→enriched→routed→confirmed→public Post A captured item carries one status the whole way. It's private through routed; only confirmed makes it public. Reshared duplicates fold into the canonical item; unwanted ones are rejected and simply leave the inbox.
